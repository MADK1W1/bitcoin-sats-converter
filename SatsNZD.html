New to Bitcoin?

Buy sats easily with NZD

Most people only hear about whole Bitcoins. What many don’t realise is that you can buy small amounts — called sats — using ordinary New Zealand dollars.

Stacked is a New Zealand based service that lets you buy and sell Bitcoin with NZD. Importantly, they never hold your coins or keys. You stay in control the whole time.

They also provide a Lightning Wallet, making sending and receiving small amounts fast and cheap.
